Review Demand and Supply Shifters

These problems use non-price shifters. A change in the good's own price would be a movement along a curve, not a shift.

Demand Shifters

  • Price of substitutes
  • Price of complements
  • Income for a normal good
  • Income for an inferior good
  • Tastes and preferences
  • Expectations of future prices
  • Expectations of future income
  • Number of buyers

Supply Shifters

  • Input prices
  • Technology
  • Expectations of future prices
  • Number of sellers
  • Weather or production shocks
  • Taxes, subsidies, or regulations that change production costs
