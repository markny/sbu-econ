Demand elasticity is usually negative because price and quantity demanded move in opposite directions.

Price Elasticity of Demand

Change any two values and solve for the third. For demand, the sign matters for direction, and the absolute value matters for elastic/unit/inelastic classification.

Result

Predicted quantity demanded changes by -15%.

Elasticity epsilon_d = -1.50
Classification Elastic
Price+10%
Quantity demanded-15%
A 10% price increase predicts a 15% decrease in quantity demanded. Since |epsilon_d| is greater than 1, demand is elastic.

Total revenue direction

Total revenue tends to fall because quantity demanded changes by a larger percentage than price.
